What is in here

  • Analytic sets are NullMeasurableSet under finite Borel measures on Polish spaces; Kechris 30.13 (capacitability for analytic sets).
  • ℝ-valued metric total variation distance with nonempty/bounded set-lemmas.
  • Binary KL with full derivative/monotonicity chain; Pinsker's inequality with the sharp constant; ℝ-valued mutual information with zero-iff-product.
  • Chebyshev-via-majority concentration bound; FintypePMF with linarith-compatible arithmetic and a bridge to Mathlib.PMF.
  • Multiplicative-weights-update (MWU) approximate minimax with T = O(log N / ε²).
  • Kernel mean embedding of a measure into an RKHS; Maximum Mean Discrepancy and IsCharacteristic; Hilbert-Schmidt Independence Criterion.
  • Assouad's dual VC bound VCdim(Mᵀ) ≤ 2^(d+1) − 1 for binary matrices.
