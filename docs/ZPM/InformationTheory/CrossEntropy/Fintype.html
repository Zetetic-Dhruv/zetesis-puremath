    Cross-entropy on FintypePMF #

    FintypePMF.crossEntropy Q P = ∑ h, Q.prob h · log (1 / P.prob h), the Fintype-indexed ℝ-valued cross-entropy. Related to KL by H(Q, P) = H(Q) + KL(Q ‖ P) where H(Q) is Shannon entropy.
