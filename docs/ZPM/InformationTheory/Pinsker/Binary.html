    Binary Pinsker inequality #

    2 (p − q)² ≤ klBin p q for p ∈ [0, 1] and q ∈ (0, 1). Case-splits on p = 0, p = 1, and p ∈ (0, 1), with the middle case further reducing to monotonicity of g on the appropriate half-interval around p.
