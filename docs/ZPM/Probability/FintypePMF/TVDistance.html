    Total variation distance between FintypePMFs (L1 form) #

    tvDistance p q = ∑ a, |p.prob a - q.prob a|. This is the L1 form of total variation; the probabilists' normalised TV is half this quantity. The L1 form is what the transfer principle composes with directly.
