6.3 Maximum Mean Discrepancy

Definition 6.6 Squared MMD

Lean: mmdSq

\(\mathrm{MMD}^2(P, Q) \; :=\; \| \mu _P - \mu _Q \| ^2\) [ 11 , Defn. 2 ] .

Theorem 6.7 Non-negativity of \(\mathrm{MMD}^2\)

Lean: mmdSq_nonneg

\(\mathrm{MMD}^2(P, Q) \ge 0\).

Theorem 6.8 \(\mathrm{MMD}^2\) on identical measures

Lean: mmdSq_self

\(\mathrm{MMD}^2(P, P) = 0\).

Theorem 6.9 Symmetry of \(\mathrm{MMD}^2\)

Lean: mmdSq_comm

\(\mathrm{MMD}^2(P, Q) = \mathrm{MMD}^2(Q, P)\).

Definition 6.10 Characteristic RKHS

Lean: IsCharacteristic

\(\mathcal{H}\) is characteristic on \(X\) iff the kernel mean embedding \(P \mapsto \mu _P\) is injective. This is the modern definition [ 20 , Defn. 6 ] .

Theorem 6.11 Zero-iff-equal under characteristic kernel

Lean: mmdSq_zero_iff

Under IsCharacteristic \(\mathcal{H}\), \(\mathrm{MMD}^2(P, Q) = 0 \iff P = Q\).
