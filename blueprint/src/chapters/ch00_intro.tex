\chapter{Introduction}\label{chap:intro}

This blueprint pins every mathematical definition and headline theorem in
\texttt{zetesis-puremath}\footnote{\url{https://github.com/Zetetic-Dhruv/zetesis-puremath}}
to its Lean~4 declaration. The companion source is a personal pure-math
reservoir: content needed by the author's formalization projects
(\texttt{formal-learning-theory-kernel}, SMFE, and others) that is either
absent from Mathlib or specialized in a way Mathlib's current formulation
does not support directly.

\section{What is formalized here}

Six substrates, each with its own chapter below:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Measure theory on Polish spaces.} Analytic sets are
    null-measurable under finite Borel measures; Kechris 30.13
    capacitability theorem for analytic sets;
    real-valued metric total variation distance.
  \item \textbf{Information theory.} Real-valued Kullback-Leibler bridge
    to Mathlib's \texttt{ENNReal}-valued \texttt{klDiv}; binary KL with a
    full derivative / monotonicity chain; Pinsker's inequality with the
    sharp constant; mutual information and its zero-iff-product
    characterization.
  \item \textbf{Probability.} \texttt{BoundedRandomVariable} typeclass
    with a Chebyshev-via-majority bound; double-sample /
    \texttt{ValidSplit} / \texttt{SplitMeasure} exchangeability
    infrastructure; \texttt{FintypePMF} (an \R-valued, \texttt{linarith}-
    compatible specialization of Mathlib's \texttt{PMF}).
  \item \textbf{Decision theory.} Covering-based minimax;
    multiplicative-weights-update (MWU) in the Freund-Schapire
    $\beta = (1-\eta)$ convention; approximate-minimax bound
    $T = \mathcal{O}(\log N / \eps^2)$.
  \item \textbf{Reproducing-kernel Hilbert spaces.} Kernel mean
    embedding with the reproducing property; Maximum Mean Discrepancy
    (MMD) and characteristic kernels; Hilbert-Schmidt Independence
    Criterion (HSIC).
  \item \textbf{Combinatorics.} Assouad's 1983 dual VC bound
    $\VCdim(M^\top) \le 2^{d+1} - 1$.
\end{itemize}
